Spaghetti Carbonara

Views: 12,345 | Category: Pasta

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g pancetta
  • 2 large eggs
  • 50g pecorino cheese, grated
  • 50g parmesan, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, peeled and chopped
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti in a large pot of boiling salted water until al dente.
  2. Fry the pancetta with the garlic until crisp.
  3. Beat the eggs in a bowl, then mix in the cheeses.
  4. Drain the pasta and return to the pot.
  5. Quickly mix in the egg and cheese mixture, stirring constantly.
  6. Season with salt and pepper, serve immediately.
